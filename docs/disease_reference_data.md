# Top 50 Clinical Diseases — Reference Dataset

This document contains the structured clinical reference data for the top 50 common diseases utilized in the DR. DOOM medical awareness engine (`backend/disease_service.py`).

> **Clinical Disclaimer:** Treatment entries describe general medical approaches and evidence-based standards, not individualized patient prescriptions. A licensed healthcare provider must always assess individual symptoms, diagnostic tests, and clinical indications.

---

| --- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Common cold             | Sneezing, runny nose                  | Rest and symptom relief                                     | Handwashing, soup, fluids                               |
| 2   | Influenza (flu)         | Fever, cough, body aches              | Symptom care; antivirals for selected patients              | Vaccination, rest, nutritious food                      |

| 6   | Typhoid fever           | Prolonged fever, abdominal pain       | Appropriate prescribed antibiotics                          | Safe water and cooked food                              |
| 7   | Tuberculosis            | Persistent cough, weight loss         | Complete prescribed multidrug treatment                     | Ventilation, respiratory precautions, protein-rich food |
| 8   | Pneumonia               | Cough, fever, breathlessness          | Treatment based on cause and severity                       | Vaccination when indicated, fluids                      |

| 11  | Urinary tract infection | Burning urination, frequent urination | Assessment and antibiotics when indicated                   | Adequate fluids, personal hygiene                       |
| 12  | Hepatitis A             | Nausea, fatigue, jaundice             | Supportive care and monitoring                              | Safe food, water and handwashing                        |
